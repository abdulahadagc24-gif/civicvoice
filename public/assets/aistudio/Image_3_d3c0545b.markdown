## Brand & Style
The design system operates at the nexus of high-velocity municipal telemetry, planetary governance, and immersive cybernetic civic infrastructure. It is designed for municipal operators, urban architects, civic delegates, and citizens interacting with real-time digital twins of urban environments.

The visual style blends **Cybernetic Glassmorphism** with **Futuristic HUD Architecture**:
- **Atmospheric Depth:** Deep-space midnight layered surfaces evoke holographic control consoles monitoring infrastructure, environmental sensors, and collective voting nodes.
- **Luminous Edge Lighting & Telemetry:** High-precision, fine vector borders infused with neon cyan and electric blue field lines emulate head-up displays (HUDs) and holographic instrumentation.
- **Decisive Civic Agency:** A palette balancing algorithmic clarity and civic legitimacy, where living municipal data feels tactical, immediate, and high-impact.

## Colors
The system relies on a stark, layered dark foundation punctuated by electric coherent light:

- **Primary (`#00F0FF` - Neon Cyan):** Represents high-priority civic telemetry, active status indicators, focused UI states, and HUD reticle accents.
- **Secondary (`#0066FF` - Electric Blue):** Drives authoritative actions, structural framing, high-density charts, and foundational interactions.
- **Tertiary (`#10B981` - Luminous Emerald):** Designates verified municipal status, stable civic infrastructure, quorum achievement, and operational health.
- **Neutral Foundation (`#050914`, `#0A0F24`, `#0D1536`):** Deep midnight obsidian hierarchy providing limitless contrast for glowing vector elements and volumetric 3D map views.

### Functional Color Applications
- **Warning / Alert (`#F59E0B`):** Urban congestion, utility maintenance warnings, pending council resolutions.
- **Critical / Emergency (`#EF4444`):** Grid failure, active emergency response protocols, civic vetoes.
- **Surface Elevation Layers:**
  - Base Floor: `#050914`
  - HUD Panel / Container: `rgba(10, 15, 36, 0.72)` with blur
  - Elevated Popover / Modal: `rgba(13, 21, 54, 0.88)` with blur
  - Cybernetic Border Accents: `rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.18)` to `rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.45)` on active focus.

## Typography
The typographical hierarchy merges technical discipline with supreme screen legibility:

- **Display & Headings (Space Grotesk):** Engineered geometry conveying mechanical precision and municipal authority. Headings feature subtle negative letter-spacing for structural tightness.
- **Body & Data Dense Context (Inter):** Highly legible grotesque structure tuned for low-glare dark displays. Maintains neutral readability across complex legislative proposals, policy briefs, and citizen feedback logs.
- **Labels & Telemetry (Space Grotesk Uppercase):** Fixed tracked uppercase treatments for geospatial coordinates, grid sector markers, live vote counts, and device telemetry headers.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout uses a multi-layered viewport paradigm engineered for both panoramic 3D twin exploration and surgical dashboard execution:

- **Grid Architecture:** 12-column adaptive fluid grid on desktop (`1440px+`) with `1.5rem` gutters and `2rem` outer margins. On tablet (`768px - 1439px`), the grid compresses to 8 columns. On mobile screens (`<768px`), layout switches to a single or dual-column stream with `1rem` margins.
- **HUD Overlays:** Persistent telemetry docks span horizontal extremes with safe zone clearances to host real-time 3D canvas viewport controls without obscuring actionable citizen components.
- **Spacing Cadence:** Based on an uncompromising 4px/8px modular base scale to guarantee rhythmic symmetry across tactical data clusters, pill indicators, and modal consoles.

## Elevation & Depth
Elevation is realized through atmospheric luminous strata and translucent optical filters rather than heavy drop shadows:

- **Level 0 (Canvases & Substrates):** Pure midnight base `#050914` underlaying geospatial 3D engines and particle simulations.
- **Level 1 (Docked Telemetry Panels):** `rgba(10, 15, 36, 0.75)` backdrop fill paired with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` and an interior 1px boundary of `rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.15)`.
- **Level 2 (Active Cards & Floating Consoles):** `rgba(13, 21, 54, 0.85)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(24px)`, framed by a dual-edge hairline border: `rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.35)` with an ambient cyan glow (`box-shadow: 0 0 20px rgba(0, 102, 255, 0.15), inset 0 1px 0 rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.2)`).
- **Level 3 (Modals & Emergency HUD Overrides):** `rgba(5, 9, 20, 0.95)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(32px)`, an outer perimeter glow (`box-shadow: 0 0 35px rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.25)`), and top accent stroke of vibrant cyan `#00F0FF`.

## Shapes
A clean **Soft (`roundedness: 1`)** geometry governs the system. Precision-cut corners retain technical rigor without the sterility of pure right angles:

- Standard structural panels, modules, and inputs leverage a crisp `0.25rem` (4px) curvature.
- Intermediate cards and floating flyouts utilize `rounded-lg` (`0.5rem` / 8px).
- Specialized HUD telemetry chips and civic status badges use full rounded pill architecture to visually demarcate runtime variables from structural viewports.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Controls
- **Primary Cyber Button:** High-intensity Neon Cyan gradient to Electric Blue (`linear-gradient(135deg, #00F0FF 0%, #0066FF 100%)`) with dark typography (`#050914`), bold weight, and a directional glow `box-shadow: 0 0 16px rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.4)`. Hover expands glow aura.
- **Secondary Ghost Action:** Translucent dark fill (`rgba(0, 102, 255, 0.1)`), 1px solid stroke (`rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.3)`), and cyan text. Active state triggers full border glow.
- **Emergency / Veto Action:** Crimson tint fill (`rgba(239, 68, 68, 0.15)`), red border (`#EF4444`), white typography.

### Telemetry Pill Tags & Badges
- Compact indicators containing an animated 6px radar pulse dot.
- Emerald Status (`rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.15)` fill, `#10B981` border, `#10B981` uppercase text) for healthy sector operations and verified ballots.
- Cyan Telemetry Tag for live sensor readings and demographic density markers.

### Input Fields & Search Consoles
- Dark obsidian base (`rgba(10, 15, 36, 0.8)`), inset 1px border (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`).
- On focus: 1px glowing cyan border (`#00F0FF`) with ambient perimeter wash (`box-shadow: 0 0 12px rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.25)`). Monospaced metadata or icon prefixes in `Space Grotesk`.

### Telemetry Cards & Metric Nodes
- Frosted glass finish, sharp internal layout, micro-grids, and sector coordinate coordinates stamped in corners (`e.g., SEC-09 // LAT: 40.7128`).
- Integrated miniature sparklines glowing in `#00F0FF` with tertiary green baseline markers.

### Checkboxes, Radios, & Toggles
- Custom angular check elements with neon fill transitions and sharp cyan borders. Checked state activates an intense core pulse.

### 3D Canvas HUD Reticles & Data Clusters
- Reticles feature segmented crosshairs, micro-bracket corners, and live streaming metrics that hover anchored over 3D municipal assets.
